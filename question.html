A visual demonstration of HTML headings and the CSS Box Model.

Heading Level 1

The H1 tag is the most important heading on the page. It is typically used for the main title and should only appear once per page for SEO best practices.

Heading Level 2

H2 tags act as subheaders for major sections of your content. They help break up long articles into digestible chunks.

Heading Level 3

H3 tags are used for subsections within an H2. They provide a deeper level of hierarchy for complex topics.

Heading Level 4

H4 is often used for small grouped items, sidebar titles, or minor section titles within a larger chapter.

Heading Level 5

H5 headings are less common but useful for detailed technical documentation or micro-sections within an application.

Heading Level 6

The smallest heading level, H6 is used for low-level organizational needs while still maintaining a semantic structure.
