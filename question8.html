Helping Hands Foundation

"Small Acts, Big Impact. Join us in making a difference."

Helping Hands is a non-profit organization dedicated to uplifting underprivileged communities through education, healthcare, and environmental sustainability. We believe that everyone has a skill that can change a life. By volunteering with us, you contribute directly to grassroots development and gain invaluable experience in social work.

Our Active Projects

  • Project Vidya: Providing free weekend tuitions to primary school children.
  • Green Earth: Weekly tree plantation and waste management workshops.
  • Annapurna: Daily community kitchen serving nutritious meals to the homeless.
  • Health First: Mobile clinics providing basic checkups in rural areas.
