Margherita Pizza

Fresh, simple, and Italian classic.

Ingredients Needed

  • 250g Pizza Dough
  • 1/2 cup Tomato San Marzano sauce
  • 100g Fresh Mozzarella cheese
  • 4-5 Fresh Basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp Extra virgin olive oil
  • A pinch of Sea salt

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 250°C (480°F) for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Roll out the dough on a floured surface to a 12-inch circle.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly, leaving 1 inch around the edge for the crust.
  4. Tear the fresh mozzarella into chunks and place them over the sauce.
  5. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle a pinch of salt.
  6. Bake for 8-10 minutes until the crust is charred and cheese is bubbly.
  7. Top with fresh basil leaves immediately after removing from the oven.
Preparation time: 15 mins | Cook time: 10 mins
